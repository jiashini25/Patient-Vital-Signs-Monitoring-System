**Algorithm: Patient Vital Signs Monitoring System**

1. Start the program.
2. Declare variables for heart rate and SpO₂.
3. Read the patient's heart rate.
4. Read the patient's SpO₂ level.
5. Display the entered readings.
6. Check whether the heart rate is outside the configured range of 60–100 bpm.
7. Check whether the SpO₂ is outside the configured range of 95–100%.
8. Display the corresponding status for each reading.
9. Stop the program.

**Note:** The ranges are illustrative programming thresholds, not a substitute for clinical assessment.
